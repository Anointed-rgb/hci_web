Who We Are • About HCI

Survivor-Led Care for Children, Adolescents & Young Adults (AYA)

Hope & Courage International (HCI) is a survivor-led, Kenya-based Non-Governmental Organization dedicated to supporting Children, Adolescents (13–18 years), and Young Adults (18–25 years) affected by childhood cancers and transitioning into survivorship.

HCI goal is “Healing through Strengthening the Body, Educating the Mind and Nurturing Hope & Courage.” Because survivorship is not just about living—it’s about living well.

Our Mission

To provide holistic psychosocial support, survivorship care, advocacy, and safe spaces for adolescents and young adults affected by cancer across Kenya.

Our Vision

A Kenya where every adolescent and young adult affected by cancer thrives with dignity, hope, and a strong support system — during treatment and throughout survivorship.

Our Story • Founded by Catherine Wambugu

From Hospital Benches to Advocacy, Mentorship & Safe Healing

“My name is Catherine Wambugu, and I am the founder of Hope & Courage International. Diagnosed with leukemia as a young adult with two relapses, I experienced sleeping on hospital benches, isolation, and uncertainty firsthand. After participating in a CAR T-cell therapy clinical trial and receiving care locally and in India, I built HCI so no child, teen, or young adult walks the cancer journey alone.”
